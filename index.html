FAQs

Questions, answered.

How much is 1M tokens worth?

About 750,000 English words. That is roughly 1,500 pages, or around 10 novels. Arabic text uses more tokens per word, so the same budget covers fewer Arabic words. Most team members use 1.5M to 2M tokens a month.

Can smaller open models perform as well as the big ones in my field?

Yes, when they are trained on the right data. A smaller model trained on your work can match much larger general-purpose models on the tasks your business actually does. It also ends up cheaper to run, faster to respond and more consistent in its answers.

How long does it take to deploy a model on LayerOS?

Your model is live within 5 minutes of registering. Pick a model, invite your team and start working the same day. No code and no setup on your side.

Can I switch models and keep what my AI has learned?

Yes. Everything your AI has learned about your business stays with you. When you move to a newer or different model, LayerOS carries that knowledge over to it, so you never start from zero.
